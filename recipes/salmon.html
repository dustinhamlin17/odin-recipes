Seared Salmon

Description

This is a deliciously easy recipe for pan seared salmon! Easily make this in under 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 4 (6 ounce) fillets Salmon
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons capers
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 4 slices lemon

Steps

  1. Preheat large skillet over medium high for 3 minutes.
  2. Coat salmon with olive oil; place skin-side down in the preheated skillet and increase heat to high
  3. Sprinkle with capers,salt,pepper;cook for 3 minutes on one side. Turn salmon fillets over;continue to cook until salmon easily flakes with fork, about 5 minutes
  4. Transfer salmon to plates and garnish with lemon slices (optional)

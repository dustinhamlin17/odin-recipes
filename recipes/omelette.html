Greek Omelette

A delicious spinach and feta filled omelette

Ingredients

  • Egg
  • Feta
  • Pepper
  • Salt
  • baby spinach

Steps

  1. Heat skillet over medium-high heat
  2. Whisk 4 eggs into a bowl with salt and pepper
  3. Add contents of bowl to pan and cook until edges begin to crisp
  4. Add feta and spinach onto one side of the omelette and fold in half
  5. Cook for 1 minute, flip the omelette and cook for another minute
  6. Serve with bacon and toast

Lazy Baked Potato

This delicious baked potato will keep you full and happy

Ingredients

  • Potato
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Rosemary
  • Shredded Cheese

Steps

  1. Using running water scrub your potatoe to remove any dirt
  2. Using a fork, poke several holes all over your potato
  3. Dry the potato with a paper towel and place in the microwave for 3 minutes
  4. Flip the potato and cook for another 3 minutes
  5. Remove from microwave and cut the potato in half with your fork
  6. Season with salt and Rosemary
  7. Add a spoonful of butter and a healthy sprinkle of shredded cheese
